# Pilíře (Pillars)

**Build your day. Watch your town grow.**

Pilíře is a gentle, gamified iOS app for building habits and getting through
ordinary days. It is not another productivity tracker — it is a small game about
your life. Each day you name a handful of things that matter. Every one you
finish earns material you spend on a 3D low-poly town growing on a floating
island that is entirely your own.

Made for people who want to build habits without pressure or guilt.
ADHD-friendly by design: it never punishes, never nags, never shames.

---

## The idea

> Finish your pillars of the day and let your town grow.

Most habit apps are built around streaks you can break and red marks you can
earn. Pilíře is built around something quieter: **you showed up, so something
grew.** Miss a day and nothing burns down — your town is still there, waiting.

---

## Pillars and tasks

**Pillars** (*pilíře*) are the few things holding up your day. Not a backlog,
not everything you could do — just what would make today count. Add them in the
morning, tick them off as you go, and close the day when you're done.

- **Finish a pillar → earn wood.** Instant, tangible reward.
- **Close the day → earn bricks and steel**, scaled to how much you got done.
- **Didn't finish something?** Carry it forward to today with one tap, or let it
  go. Nothing sits in your history glaring at you as a failure.
- **Need a nudge?** Ask for a random task from a pool of small, doable ones.
- **Doing it every day?** Turn any task into a recurring habit with its own time
  and a single quiet reminder — never a stream of them.

Tasks aren't a debt you owe. They're the material your town is made of.

## What's in the game

- **A 3D town on a floating island** — cottages, farms, a tavern with a beer
  garden, a school, a church, a castle, a cathedral. You choose what to build
  and where, and you can move anything later.
- **Momentum** — a small, honest measure of how regularly you show up. It grows
  by one for every day you open the app and drops by one for every day you miss.
  It multiplies your rewards. It never shames you.
- **A living map** — real day/night lighting, drifting clouds that cast shadows,
  birds flying over, butterflies by day and fireflies at night, weather, smoke
  from chimneys, a working fountain.
- **A calendar** in the spirit of a habit streak board: days tinted by how they
  went, and a **weekly chest** with a surprise reward when you show up every day
  of the week.
- **A chronicle** — every day you closed and what you built with it.
- **A zoo** — dogs, a husky, a fox, a wolf, a deer, a cow, a horse, an alpaca.
  They roam the island on their own, walk around your buildings, sleep at night
  (except the night owls), and you can pick them up and move them. Build a
  doghouse, put your dog's name on it, and fill the bowl.
- **Decorations** — roads you paint by dragging, trees, fruit trees, bushes,
  benches, a pond, a fire pit that lights itself after dark, a bird feeder that
  birds actually visit.

## Built with

- **Swift 6 / SwiftUI** — iOS 26+
- **SceneKit** — the isometric 3D town, all buildings and decorations generated
  procedurally in code
- **SwiftData** — local persistence, no account, no server, no tracking
- **EventKit** — optional export to Apple Calendar and Reminders
- **[GLTFKit2](https://github.com/warrenm/GLTFKit2)** (MIT) — loads the rigged
  animal models

### Assets

Animal models from the **[Quaternius](https://quaternius.com) Ultimate Animated
Animals** pack, released under **CC0** (public domain). Everything else —
buildings, decorations, terrain, the app icon — is generated or drawn for this
project.

---

## Privacy

Everything stays on your device. No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no
network calls. Calendar and Reminders access is optional and only ever writes.
