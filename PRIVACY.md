# Privacy Policy — Pilíře

_Last updated: 26 July 2026_

**Short version: Pilíře collects nothing. Everything you write stays on your
phone. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.**

---

## What data the app stores

Pilíře stores the following **only on your device**, in the app's own private
storage:

- The pillars (tasks) and habits you write
- Which days you closed and what you completed
- Your town: buildings, decorations, animals and their names
- Your coins, materials and momentum
- App preferences (reminder times, map controls, whether notifications are on)

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded, synced, backed up to us,
or shared with anyone.

## What we collect

Nothing.

- **No account.** There is no sign-up and no login.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app contains no analytics SDK, no
  advertising identifier, and no crash-reporting service.
- **No network requests.** The app does not contact any server. It works fully
  offline.
- **No third-party data sharing.** There is nobody to share with.

## Permissions the app may ask for

Each of these is optional. The app works without them, and you can revoke any of
them in iOS Settings at any time.

- **Notifications** — used only for local reminders you set up yourself
  (a habit's time, a scheduled task, the daily nudges). Reminders are scheduled
  on your device; nothing is sent through a server.
- **Calendar (write only)** — used only if you ask the app to add a scheduled
  task to Apple Calendar. The app has *write-only* access: it can add an event
  but cannot read your calendar.
- **Reminders** — used only if you ask the app to mirror a habit into Apple
  Reminders.

## Deleting your data

Settings → **Smazat všechna data** (Delete all data) removes everything the app
has stored. Deleting the app from your device also removes all of it.

If iCloud Backup is enabled on your iPhone, your device backup may include the
app's data, as it does for any app. That backup is between you and Apple; we
have no access to it.

## Children

Pilíře does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
